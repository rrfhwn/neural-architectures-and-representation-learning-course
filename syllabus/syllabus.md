# Neural Architectures and Representation Learning

## Course Description

This Master-level course covers theoretical and practical foundations of Deep Representation Learning, including modern neural architectures and MLOps workflows.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students:

- can explain fundamental concepts of (Deep) Representation Learning, including Linear Threshold Units (LTU), Perceptron, and Multilayer Perceptron, and implement simple applications.
- are able to design and train neural networks with appropriate activation functions and initialization strategies (PyTorch, Optuna), and improve pretrained models using modern optimization methods (e.g., PEFT).
- can apply advanced regularization techniques such as Dropout, Batch Normalization, and Gradient Clipping to reduce overfitting and improve generalization.
- can analyze, implement (PyTorch), and apply different architectures including CNNs, RNNs, and Transformers to domain-specific applications (e.g., computer vision, time-series analysis, NLP).
- can critically assess current developments and trends in Deep Representation Learning and evaluate their impact on academia and industry.
- can design ML workflows according to MLOps principles, including:
  - code and data versioning (Git, DVC)
  - experiment tracking (MLflow, Weights & Biases)
  - hyperparameter optimization (Optuna, Ray Tune)
  - deployment (Docker, FastAPI, TorchServe)

---

## Central Topics

- Fundamentals of (Deep) Representation Learning  
- Practical modeling and optimization (e.g., Optuna)  
- Pretrained models, fine-tuning, and transfer learning  
- Interpretability (e.g., Activation Atlas) and fairness  
- MLOps for representation learning models

## Assessment

The course grade is based on:

**5 practical notebooks (each 20% of the final grade).**

The instructor reserves the right to conduct oral follow-up discussions to verify independent work and understanding.

Points are awarded only if students can clearly explain and justify their submitted solutions.
